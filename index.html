About Innovative Insights

Publishing Knowledge. Expanding Perspectives.

“Innovative Insights (SMC-Private) Limited publishes open access, peer-reviewed journals, edited books and conference proceedings.”

We started with a simple goal: to give researchers, especially those in emerging and developing economies, a credible place to share work on the social, economic, legal and technological questions that matter to their communities.

Each of our journals is run by its own editorial board, which makes its decisions independently. Everything we publish is released under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) license.

Scholarly Governance & Standards

Integrity at Every Stage of Publishing.

Our publishing practices are built around accessibility, transparency, academic independence, and responsible scholarly communication.

Every article is free to read online, without subscriptions, sign-ups, or paywalls. Innovative follows the Diamond Open Access model: reading and publishing are both free.

Every article we publish is free to read online, with no subscriptions, sign-ups or paywalls.
We follow a Diamond Open Access model, which means it costs nothing to read our journals and nothing to publish in them.

Author Sovereignty & Editorial Autonomy

Research Belongs to Its Authors.

Scholarly communication works best when authors retain ownership of their intellectual output and editorial decisions remain insulated from commercial interest.

Principle 01

Author Ownership

Authors retain full copyright over their published work. You simply grant the journal the right to publish your work first under the CC BY 4.0 license.

Full copyright retained
Principle 02

Freedom to Share

Authors can share their preprints, accepted manuscripts, and final published versions immediately upon publication with no embargo period and no additional permission.

Zero-embargo self-archiving
Principle 03

Independent Decisions

Each journal operates through its own editorial board, and publication decisions are based on academic merit alone, completely free from commercial or organizational bias.

Editorial autonomy guaranteed
Financial Model & Transparency

How Our Operations Are Funded

Since authors don't pay to publish, we keep our journals running through three sources: support from Innovative Insights (SMC-Private) Limited itself, and optional purchases of printed issues by authors. None of these has any say in what gets published. Editorial decisions are made on academic merit alone.
